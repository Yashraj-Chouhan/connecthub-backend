How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Select one of the featured packs or enter a custom amount.
  2. The payment service creates a Razorpay order and opens secure checkout for that amount.
  3. After payment, the backend verifies the Razorpay signature before credits are added.
Credits are never added from the browser alone. The payment service checks the payment record before syncing your wallet.
